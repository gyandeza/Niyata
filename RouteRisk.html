Methodology

Explainable scoring for high-stakes operational risk.

Every recommendation should be inspectable. RouteRisk displays the score components, weighting, and operational recommendation behind each route.

Formula

Weighted Risk Score = Sum(component risk x component weight)

Scores below 35 are low risk, 35-69 require monitoring, and 70+ require delay, reroute, or managerial approval.
